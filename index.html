ℹ️ About this project

This is the first working prototype, not a claim that every item has already been catalogued. The data model supports a much larger inventory. Records will be expanded from NASA mission histories, NASA technical catalogues, image archives and the official lunar-artifacts catalogue.

01Separate object from mission. A rover, a lander, its heat shield, parachute and descent stage can be distinct records when they are distinct pieces of hardware.
02Label status carefully. Ended mission, surface remnant, intentional impact, and ongoing scientific use are not interchangeable.
03Show the source on every card. Follow the NASA link to check dates, context, image credit and mission details.
04Never pass artwork off as evidence. The cover is conceptual art; image captions clarify when a NASA image is a reference view rather than a photograph of the exact discarded object.
